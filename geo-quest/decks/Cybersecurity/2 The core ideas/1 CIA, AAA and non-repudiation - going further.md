Name the goal at stake and the step that failed.

## Which goal?
- Ransomware encrypts a hospital's records so staff can't open them: the attack hits {{availability|which goal?}} first.
- A database of customer emails is posted online: a breach of {{confidentiality|which goal?}}.
- An attacker quietly changes the bank account number on an invoice: an attack on {{integrity|which goal?}}.
- A flood of junk traffic takes a website offline: a {{denial-of-service|what kind of?}} attack on availability.

## Which step?
- A user logs in fine but can open the payroll folder they shouldn't: a failure of {{authorization|which step?}}.
- After a breach, nobody can tell which account deleted the files: the logs, {{accounting|which step?}}, were missing.
- Three staff share one admin password, so nobody can be held to an action: there's no {{non-repudiation|what?}}.

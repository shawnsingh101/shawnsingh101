Classify the control and read the risk.

## Classify it
- A company can't patch an old machine, so it isolates it on its own network: a {{compensating|which type?}} control.
- Reviewing the firewall logs every morning is an {{operational|which category?}} control of the detective type.
- A camera that records a break-in is {{detective|which type?}}; a sign saying "CCTV in use" is a deterrent.

## Read the risk
- A server is unpatched but unplugged from every network: the vulnerability is real, but the {{likelihood|which part of risk?}} is near zero.
- A user on the office network asks for the finance share from an unmanaged personal laptop; zero trust {{denies or limits|does what?}} it, because the device can't be verified.

## Scenarios
- A US online shop sells to customers in France: it must follow the {{GDPR|which law?}}.
- A small dental office stores patient records: {{HIPAA|which law?}} applies.
- An accountant's fraud surfaces while she's on her required two weeks off: mandatory {{vacation|what?}} working.
- A server change made on Friday night with no approval breaks the website: a failure of change {{management|what?}}.
- A cloud provider stores a German company's data in the United States: a question of data {{sovereignty|what?}}.

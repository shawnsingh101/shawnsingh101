Choose the model and find the flaw.

## Scenarios
- A hospital gives every nurse the same permissions through one group: {{role-based|which model?}} access control.
- Finance staff can reach the payment system only from company laptops, during work hours, from the office: {{attribute-based|which model?}} access control.
- A developer who moved to sales still has access to the code repository: {{privilege creep|what problem?}}, found by an access review.
- A calendar app asks to "see your Google calendar" and never learns your Google password: that's {{OAuth|which standard?}}.
- An attacker copies a Kerberos credential from memory and uses it without the password: a pass-the-{{ticket|what?}} attack.

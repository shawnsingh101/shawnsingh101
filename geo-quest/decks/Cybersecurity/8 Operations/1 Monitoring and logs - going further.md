## Scenarios
- Events from two servers seem to happen in an impossible order: their clocks are out of sync; fix {{NTP (network time protocol)|what?}}.
- An employee emails a spreadsheet of customer card numbers to a personal account and it's blocked: {{DLP (data loss prevention)|what?}}.
- The SIEM raises 4,000 alerts a day and the team reads none: {{tune|do what to?}} the rules.
- A user logs in from New York and, ten minutes later, from Singapore: an {{impossible travel|what kind of?}} alert.
- A system file in Windows changes outside any update: file integrity monitoring flags it as a possible {{rootkit|what?}}.

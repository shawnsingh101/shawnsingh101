title: What archaea are
---
The second domain: single cells with no nucleus, like bacteria, yet as different from them as from us. Many thrive where nothing else can.

## A third domain
- In 1977 {{Carl Woese|who?}} compared their RNA and found that archaea are not bacteria but a domain of their own.
- The name archaea means {{“ancient ones”|what?}}, in Greek.
- Our own ancestor, the first cell with a nucleus, likely arose from an archaeon related to today's {{Asgard|which?}} archaea, named for the realm of the Norse gods.

## Living at the extremes
- Archaea that thrive in near-boiling water are called {{thermophiles|what?}}, “heat lovers”.
![Grand Prismatic Spring, Yellowstone: its rings of color are mats of microbes, each suited to a different temperature](commons:Grand Prismatic Spring and Midway Geyser Basin from above.jpg)
- Only archaea make {{methane|which gas?}} as they live: the methanogens in cows' stomachs, rice paddies and swamps.
- No archaeon is known to cause {{disease|what?}}.

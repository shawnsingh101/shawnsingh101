The world's clubs on the map: picture who belongs to NATO, the EU, BRICS, OPEC and the rest, then see them light up; or see a group lit up and name it. Membership changes (Sweden joined NATO in 2024, Indonesia BRICS in 2025, Bulgaria the euro in 2026), so these cards carry a date and the member lists are checked every Wednesday.

:::lesson
## Three kinds of club
- **Alliances** promise to defend each other: NATO (an attack on one is an attack on all), the CSTO around Russia, AUKUS and the Quad in the Pacific.
- **Economic blocs** remove barriers between members: the European Union (one market, and for 21 of its 27 one currency), the USMCA, Mercosur, ASEAN.
- **Forums** have no treaty power, only meetings and pressure: the G7, the G20, BRICS. A cartel, OPEC, coordinates how much oil its members pump.

## Overlaps worth knowing
- **NATO ≠ EU.** 23 countries are in both. Norway, Iceland, the UK, Turkey, Canada, the US, Albania, Montenegro and North Macedonia are NATO but not EU; Austria, Ireland, Malta and Cyprus are EU but not NATO.
- **Schengen ≠ EU.** No border checks among 29 countries: not Ireland or Cyprus, but yes Norway, Iceland, Switzerland and Liechtenstein.
- **The euro ≠ EU.** 21 of 27; Denmark has an opt-out, and Sweden, Poland, Czechia, Hungary and Romania keep their own currencies for now.
:::

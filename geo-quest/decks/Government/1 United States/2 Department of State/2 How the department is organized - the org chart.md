Who reports to whom at State, drawn as blocks: each under secretary and the bureaus under them, and the offices that answer straight to the Secretary. From the department's own organization chart (June 2026), a public-domain US government work. Each chart asks for one box at a time.

:::lesson
## The top of the chart
```org
# The front office
Secretary of State (S)
  Counselor (C) | beside the Secretary
  Chief of Staff (COS) | beside the Secretary
  Deputy Secretary of State (D) | the number two
  Deputy Secretary for Management and Resources (D-MR) | the number three
  Executive Secretariat (S/ES) | Executive Secretary; moves paper to and from the Secretary
```

Below the two deputies hang **six under secretaries**, each known by a letter, and under each a family of bureaus. Most bureaus are run by an **assistant secretary**; a few by a director, an ambassador-at-large, or (for the money) a comptroller.

## The six families
```org
# Under secretaries and their bureaus (June 2026)
Under Secretary for Political Affairs (P)
  AF · EAP · EUR · NEA · SCA · WHA | the six regional bureaus
  International Organizations (IO)
Under Secretary for Economic Affairs (E)
  EEB · OES · CDP | economy and business; oceans and science; cyberspace
  Office of the Chief Economist (OCE)
Under Secretary for Arms Control and International Security (T)
  ACN · PM · INL · CT · ET | arms control; political-military; narcotics; counterterrorism; emerging threats
Under Secretary for Public Diplomacy and Public Affairs (R)
  ECA · GPA | exchanges; global public affairs
Under Secretary for Management (M)
  CA · DS · OBO · HR · A · BP · CGFS · DT · GA · MED · OFM | eleven bureaus and offices that run the machine
Under Secretary for Foreign Assistance, Humanitarian Affairs, and Religious Freedom (F)
  DRL · PRM · DHR · GHSD · FAO | rights; refugees; disasters; global health; aid oversight
```

Two surprises on the June 2026 chart: **International Narcotics and Law Enforcement (INL)** and **Counterterrorism (CT)** now sit under **T**, the security under secretary, and the Bureau of **Disaster and Humanitarian Response (DHR)**, the home of the old USAID disaster teams, sits under **F**.

## Straight to the Secretary
Seven offices sit outside the families and answer to the Secretary: Intelligence and Research (INR), the Inspector General (OIG), Legislative Affairs (H), the Legal Adviser (L), Policy Planning (S/P), the Chief of Protocol (S/CPR), and Civil Rights and Ombudsman (OCR). By law, the heads of **Diplomatic Security** and **Diplomatic Technology** also report directly to the Secretary for some purposes, though they sit under M.
:::

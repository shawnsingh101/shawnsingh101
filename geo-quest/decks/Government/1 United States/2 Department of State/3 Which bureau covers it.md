title: Which bureau covers it
---
Every embassy reports to one of six regional bureaus. Name the bureau from the country on the map; flipping colors the whole world by bureau. The tricky borders come first: North Africa, Turkey, the Caucasus, Central Asia, Canada.

## The tricky borders
- [bureau: EGY] Which regional bureau covers Egypt?
> Egypt is in Africa, but North Africa from Morocco to Egypt belongs to Near Eastern Affairs, with the Arab world.
- [bureau: MAR] Which regional bureau covers Morocco?
- [bureau: LBY] Which regional bureau covers Libya?
- [bureau: SDN] Which regional bureau covers Sudan?
> Sudan borders Egypt, but it is covered by African Affairs.
- [bureau: MRT] Which regional bureau covers Mauritania?
- [bureau: TUR] Which regional bureau covers Turkey?
> Turkey is a NATO ally, so it is handled with Europe.
- [bureau: GEO] Which regional bureau covers Georgia?
- [bureau: AZE] Which regional bureau covers Azerbaijan?
- [bureau: CYP] Which regional bureau covers Cyprus?
- [bureau: IRN] Which regional bureau covers Iran?
- [bureau: AFG] Which regional bureau covers Afghanistan?
- [bureau: KAZ] Which regional bureau covers Kazakhstan?
> The five Central Asian countries are grouped with South Asia, not with Russia.
- [bureau: UZB] Which regional bureau covers Uzbekistan?
- [bureau: CAN] Which regional bureau covers Canada?
> Canada, Mexico and every country to Argentina are one bureau.
- [bureau: MNG] Which regional bureau covers Mongolia?
- [bureau: MMR] Which regional bureau covers Burma (Myanmar)?
> Burma is the name the US government uses.
- [bureau: TWN] Which regional bureau covers Taiwan?

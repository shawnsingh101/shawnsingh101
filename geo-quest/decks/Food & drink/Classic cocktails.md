Example deck: eight classics and how they're built. Replace or extend it with your own.

## Negroni
![Negroni](commons:Negroni served in Vancouver BC.jpg)
- A Negroni is equal parts {{gin}}, {{Campari}} and {{sweet vermouth}}.
- Negroni garnish :: an orange peel
> Stirred, served over ice.

## Old Fashioned
![Old Fashioned](commons:Whiskey Old Fashioned1.jpg)
- An Old Fashioned is {{bourbon or rye whiskey}}, a little {{sugar}} and {{Angostura bitters}}.
- Old Fashioned garnish :: an orange peel (often with a cherry)

## Manhattan
![Manhattan](commons:Manhattan Cocktail2.jpg)
- A Manhattan is {{rye whiskey}}, {{sweet vermouth}} and {{Angostura bitters}}.
- Manhattan garnish :: a cherry
> Stirred and served up. Swap in dry vermouth and a lemon twist and it's a Dry Manhattan.

## Martini
![Martini](commons:Dry Martini-2.jpg)
- A classic Martini is {{gin}} and {{dry vermouth}}, stirred.
- Martini garnish :: an olive or a lemon twist

## Daiquiri
![Daiquiri](commons:Daiquiri, Floridita bar, Havana, Cuba.jpg)
- A Daiquiri is {{white rum}}, {{lime juice}} and {{simple syrup}}, shaken.

## Margarita
![Margarita](commons:MargaritaReal.jpg)
- A Margarita is {{tequila}}, {{triple sec (Cointreau)}} and {{lime juice}}, shaken.
- Margarita glass rim :: salt

## Mojito
![Mojito](commons:MojitoReydelmojito.jpg)
- A Mojito is {{white rum}}, {{lime juice}}, sugar, {{mint}} and topped with {{soda water}}.

## Sidecar
![Sidecar](commons:Sidecar-cocktail.jpg)
- A Sidecar is {{cognac}}, {{Cointreau}} and {{lemon juice}}, shaken.
- Sidecar glass rim :: sugar

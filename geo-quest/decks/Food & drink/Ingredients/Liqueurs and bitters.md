The flavours that turn a spirit into a cocktail: what each liqueur is made from, and where it comes from.

## Campari
related: Italy, Milan
![Bitter oranges](commons:Turunç - Citrus aurantium 02.JPG)
- Campari is a bitter red liqueur from {{Milan}}, {{Italy}}, flavoured with herbs and bitter orange.
- Campari, gin and sweet vermouth in equal parts :: a Negroni

## Aperol
related: Italy, Padua
- Aperol is Campari's sweeter, orange cousin, first made in {{Padua}}, flavoured with bitter orange and {{rhubarb}}.
- Aperol, prosecco and a splash of soda :: an Aperol spritz

## Triple sec and Cointreau
related: France
![Bitter oranges](commons:Turunç - Citrus aurantium 02.JPG)
- Cointreau is a triple sec: a clear liqueur made from {{orange peels}}, sweet and bitter.
- Cointreau is made in {{France}}.

## Amaretto
related: Italy, Saronno
![Apricot kernels](commons:Apricot kernel centimeter scale.png)
- Amaretto tastes of almonds but is often made from {{apricot kernels}}.
- Amaretto's most famous brand comes from the town of {{Saronno}}, Italy.

## Kahlúa
related: Mexico, Veracruz
![Coffee beans](commons:Roasted coffee beans.jpg)
- Kahlúa is a {{coffee}} liqueur made with rum, from {{Mexico}}.
- Vodka, coffee liqueur and cream :: a White Russian

## Chartreuse
related: France
- Chartreuse is made by {{Carthusian monks}} in the French Alps from a secret recipe of about {{130}} herbs.
- The only liqueur whose colour is named after it (green and yellow) :: Chartreuse

## Maraschino
related: Croatia, Zadar
![Marasca cherries](commons:Illustration Prunus cerasus0.jpg)
- Maraschino liqueur is made from sour {{Marasca cherries}}, first in {{Zadar}} on the coast of Croatia.

## Limoncello
related: Italy, Amalfi Coast
![Lemons](commons:Citrus x limon - Köhler–s Medizinal-Pflanzen-041.jpg)
- Limoncello is made from {{lemon zest}} soaked in spirit, from the {{Amalfi Coast}} of Italy.

## Crème de cassis
related: France, Burgundy
![Blackcurrants](commons:Schwarzejohannisbeere.jpg)
- Crème de cassis is made from {{blackcurrants}}, in {{Burgundy}}, France.
- White wine with a splash of crème de cassis :: a Kir

## Vermouth
related: Italy, Turin, France
![Wormwood](commons:Artemisia absinthium P1210748.jpg)
- Vermouth is {{wine}} flavoured with herbs; its name comes from the German for {{wormwood}}.
- Sweet (red) vermouth was born in {{Turin}}, Italy; dry vermouth in {{France}}.

## Angostura bitters
related: Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela
![Gentian](commons:Gentiana lutea 230705.jpg)
- Angostura bitters get their bitterness mainly from {{gentian}} root.
- Angostura bitters were invented in the town of Angostura, {{Venezuela}}, and are now made in {{Trinidad and Tobago}}.
- The bitters in an Old Fashioned and a Manhattan :: Angostura

## Grenadine and orgeat
![Pomegranate](commons:Punica granatum 004.JPG)
- Grenadine is a red syrup first made from {{pomegranates}} (grenade in French).
- Orgeat, the syrup in a Mai Tai, is made from {{almonds}}.

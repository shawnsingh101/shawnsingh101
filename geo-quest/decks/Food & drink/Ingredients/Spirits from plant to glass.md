Every spirit on the bar starts as a plant somewhere in the world. Learn what each one is made from, see the plant, and follow the link to where it comes from on the map.

## Gin
related: Netherlands, England
![Juniper berries](commons:Jeneverbes-spice.jpg)
- Gin gets its flavour from {{juniper berries}}.
- Gin grew out of {{jenever}}, a Dutch spirit, and became a craze in {{London}} in the 1700s.

## Tequila
related: Mexico, Jalisco
![Agave](commons:Agave americana R01.jpg)
- Tequila is made from the {{blue agave}} plant.
- Real tequila can only come from {{Mexico}}, mostly the state of {{Jalisco}}.
> Mezcal is also made from agave, of many kinds; its heart is roasted in pits, which makes it smoky. Most comes from Oaxaca.

## Rum
related: Caribbean, Barbados, Jamaica, Cuba
![Sugarcane](commons:Cut sugarcane.jpg)
- Rum is made from {{sugarcane}}, usually its {{molasses}}.
- Rum was born on the sugar plantations of the {{Caribbean}} in the 1600s.

## Cachaça
related: Brazil
- Cachaça is made from fresh {{sugarcane juice}}, not molasses, and comes from {{Brazil}}.
- The national cocktail of Brazil, made with cachaça, lime and sugar :: the caipirinha

## Bourbon
related: Kentucky, United States
![Maize](commons:Étale de grains de maïs 01.jpg)
- Bourbon is made mostly from {{corn}} (at least 51%) and aged in new charred {{oak}} barrels.
- Most bourbon is made in {{Kentucky}}.

## Rye whiskey
related: United States, Canada
![Rye](commons:Ear of rye.jpg)
- Rye whiskey is made mostly from {{rye}}, which makes it spicier than bourbon.

## Scotch whisky
related: Scotland, United Kingdom
![Barley](commons:Champ d'Orge carrée (Hordeum vulgare).jpg)
- Scotch whisky is made from malted {{barley}}, and must be aged at least {{three}} years in Scotland.
> Peat smoke used to dry the malt gives Islay whiskies their smoky taste.

## Vodka
related: Russia, Poland
native: водка (Russian); wódka (Polish)
![Potatoes](commons:234 Solanum tuberosum L.jpg)
- Vodka can be made from {{grain}} or {{potatoes}}, distilled until it tastes of almost nothing.
- Vodka's name means "little {{water}}".

## Cognac
related: France, Cognac
![Grapes](commons:Wine grapes03.jpg)
- Cognac is a brandy: a spirit distilled from {{wine}}, made from {{grapes}}.
- Cognac must come from around the town of {{Cognac}} in western {{France}}.

## Pisco
related: Peru, Chile
- Pisco is a grape brandy from {{Peru}} and {{Chile}}, both of which claim it.
- Pisco, lime, sugar and egg white, shaken :: a pisco sour

## Baijiu
related: China
native: 白酒 (Chinese)
![Sorghum](commons:Sorghum bicolor03.jpg)
- Baijiu, the most drunk spirit in the world, is made mostly from {{sorghum}} in {{China}}.

## Soju
related: South Korea
native: 소주 (Korean)
![Rice](commons:White, Brown, Red & Wild rice.jpg)
- Soju is a clear spirit from {{Korea}}, first made from {{rice}}.

## Absinthe
related: Switzerland, France
![Wormwood](commons:Artemisia absinthium P1210748.jpg)
- Absinthe gets its name and its bitterness from {{wormwood}}, with anise for its licorice taste.
- Absinthe was first made in {{Switzerland}} and became the drink of Paris's artists in the 1800s.

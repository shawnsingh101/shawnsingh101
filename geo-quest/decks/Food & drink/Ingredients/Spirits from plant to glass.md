Every spirit on the bar starts as a plant somewhere in the world. Learn what each one is made from, see the plant, and follow the link to where it comes from on the map.

## Gin
related: Netherlands, England
species: Juniperus communis
![Juniper berries](commons:Jeneverbes-spice.jpg)
- Gin gets its flavor from {{juniper berries|which berries?}}.
- Gin grew out of {{jenever|what spirit?}}, a Dutch spirit, and became a craze in {{London|what city?}} in the 1700s.

## Tequila
related: Mexico, Jalisco
species: Agave tequilana
![Agave](commons:Agave americana R01.jpg)
- Tequila is made from the {{blue agave|which plant?}} plant.
- Real tequila can only come from {{Mexico|which country?}}, mostly the state of {{Jalisco|which state?}}.
native: Mexico = México (Spanish); Jalisco = Jalisco (Spanish)
> Mezcal is also made from agave, of many kinds; its heart is roasted in pits, which makes it smoky. Most comes from Oaxaca.

## Rum
related: Caribbean, Barbados, Jamaica, Cuba
species: Saccharum officinarum
![Sugarcane](commons:Cut sugarcane.jpg)
- Rum is made from {{sugarcane|which plant?}}, usually its {{molasses|which part?}}.
- Rum was born on the sugar plantations of the {{Caribbean|which region?}} in the 1600s.

## Cachaça
related: Brazil
species: Saccharum officinarum
- Cachaça is made from fresh {{sugarcane juice|what?}}, not molasses, and comes from {{Brazil|which country?}}.
native: Brazil = Brasil (Portuguese)
- Brazil's national cocktail, made with cachaça, lime and sugar, is the {{caipirinha|which cocktail?}}.

## Bourbon
related: Kentucky, United States
species: Zea mays
![Maize](commons:Étale de grains de maïs 01.jpg)
- Bourbon is made mostly from {{corn|which grain?}} (at least 51%) and aged in new charred {{oak|which wood?}} barrels.
- Most bourbon is made in {{Kentucky|which state?}}.

## Rye whiskey
related: United States, Canada
species: Secale cereale
![Rye](commons:Ear of rye.jpg)
- Rye whiskey is made mostly from {{rye|which grain?}}, which makes it spicier than bourbon.

## Scotch whisky
related: Scotland, United Kingdom
species: Hordeum vulgare
![Barley](commons:Champ d'Orge carrée (Hordeum vulgare).jpg)
- Scotch whisky is made from malted {{barley|which grain?}}, and must be aged at least {{three|how many?}} years in Scotland.
> Peat smoke used to dry the malt gives Islay whiskies their smoky taste.

## Vodka
related: Russia, Poland
species: Solanum tuberosum (potato)
![Potatoes](commons:234 Solanum tuberosum L.jpg)
- Vodka can be made from {{grain|what?}} or {{potatoes|what?}}, distilled until it tastes of almost nothing.
- Vodka's name means "little {{water|what word?}}".

## Cognac
related: France, Cognac
species: Vitis vinifera
![Grapes](commons:Wine grapes03.jpg)
- Cognac is a brandy: a spirit distilled from {{wine|what?}}, made from {{grapes|which fruit?}}.
- Cognac must come from around the town of Cognac in western {{France|which country?}}.
native: France = France (French)

## Pisco
related: Peru, Chile
species: Vitis vinifera
- Pisco is a grape brandy from {{Peru|which country?}} and {{Chile|which country?}}, both of which claim it.
native: Peru = Perú (Spanish); Chile = Chile (Spanish)
- Pisco, lime, sugar and egg white, shaken, make a {{pisco sour|which cocktail?}}.

## Baijiu
related: China
species: Sorghum bicolor
![Sorghum](commons:Sorghum bicolor03.jpg)
- Baijiu, the most drunk spirit in the world, is made mostly from {{sorghum|which grain?}} in {{China|which country?}}.
native: China = 中国 Zhōngguó (Chinese)

## Soju
related: South Korea
species: Oryza sativa
![Rice](commons:White, Brown, Red & Wild rice.jpg)
- Soju is a clear spirit from {{Korea|which country?}}, first made from {{rice|which grain?}}.
native: Korea = 한국 Hanguk (Korean)

## Absinthe
related: Switzerland, France
species: Artemisia absinthium
![Wormwood](commons:Artemisia absinthium P1210748.jpg)
- Absinthe gets its name and its bitterness from {{wormwood|which herb?}}, with anise for its licorice taste.
- Absinthe was first made in {{Switzerland|which country?}} and became the drink of Paris's artists in the 1800s.

Layered shots are poured heaviest first, each layer floated over the back of a spoon, about 1½ oz in all, never mixed.
Shots without cream can be shaken and strained instead.

## Layering
- A layered shot goes heaviest on the {{bottom|top or bottom?}}, lightest on top.
- Each layer is poured over the back of a {{spoon|what?}}, so it floats.
- A layered shot holds about {{1½ oz|how much?}} in all.

## B-52
![A B-52](commons:Cocktail B52.jpg)
- The layers of a B-52, bottom to top, equal parts:
```parts
Coffee liqueur :: Kahlúa
Irish cream :: Baileys
Orange liqueur :: Grand Marnier
```

## After Five
- The layers of an After Five, bottom to top, equal parts:
```parts
Coffee liqueur :: Kahlúa
Irish cream :: Baileys
Mint :: Peppermint schnapps
```

## Slippery and Buttery
- A Slippery Nipple is Baileys layered with {{Sambuca|which liqueur?}}, half and half.
- A Buttery Nipple is Baileys layered with {{butterscotch schnapps|which liqueur?}}, half and half.

## Sambuca con la mosca
native: con la mosca = con la mosca (Italian)
- A shot of Sambuca is served with {{three coffee beans|what?}} floating on top, sometimes lit to roast them.
> Three beans for health, happiness and prosperity.

## Shaken shots
- A Kamikaze shooter is vodka, triple sec and {{lime juice|which juice?}}, equal parts, shaken and strained.
- A Sex on the Beach shooter is vodka, peach schnapps and {{orange juice|which juice?}}, equal parts.

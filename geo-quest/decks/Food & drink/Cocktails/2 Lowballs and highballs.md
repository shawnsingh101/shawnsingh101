title: Lowballs and highballs
---
Lowballs are a spirit on the rocks, often with a liqueur in about equal measure, stirred in a short glass. Highballs are
one shot of liquor filled with a mixer (about 1 to 4) in a tall glass, and the mixer sets the flavor. Each drink is two
cards: build it (each block is an ingredient and how much), then serve it (method, glass, garnish).

## The rules
- A lowball is poured spirit {{first|first or last?}}, then stirred, in a short glass.
- A highball is about {{1 part liquor to 4 parts mixer|what ratio?}}, whatever the size of the glass.
- In a highball, the {{mixer|what?}} sets the flavor of the drink.

## Old Fashioned
![Old Fashioned](commons:Whiskey Old Fashioned1.jpg)
related: Louisville, Kentucky
- An Old Fashioned
```recipe
sweet: ½ tsp bar sugar
bitters: A few drops of Angostura bitters
top: A splash of club soda
spirit: 2 oz bourbon
method: Muddle the sugar, bitters and soda, add ice, then whiskey; stir
glass: Lowball
garnish: A cherry and an orange slice
```

## Negroni
![Negroni](commons:Cóctel Negroni Campari.jpg)
related: Italy, Florence
- A Negroni
```recipe
spirit: 1 oz gin
liqueur: 1 oz Campari
vermouth: 1 oz sweet vermouth
method: Stirred over ice
glass: Lowball
garnish: An orange peel
```
> Equal parts. Named for Count Camillo Negroni, Florence, about 1919.

## Black Russian
![Black Russian](commons:Black Russian.jpg)
- A Black Russian
```recipe
spirit: 1 shot vodka
liqueur: 1 shot Kahlúa
method: Poured over ice, stirred
glass: Lowball
garnish: None
```
> Add cream and shake, and it's a White Russian.

## Mint Julep
![Mint Julep](commons:Mint Julep im Silberbecher.jpg)
related: Kentucky, Louisville
- A Mint Julep
```recipe
other: 10 to 15 fresh mint leaves
sweet: 1 tsp sugar
top: 1 tbsp water
spirit: 2 oz bourbon
method: Muddle the mint, sugar and water; add ice and bourbon
glass: Lowball (or a silver cup)
garnish: Mint sprigs
```
> The drink of the Kentucky Derby.

## Mojito
![Mojito](commons:15-09-26-RalfR-WLC-0072.jpg)
related: Cuba, Havana
- A Mojito
```recipe
other: 10 to 15 fresh mint leaves
sweet: 1 tsp sugar
sour: 1 tbsp lime juice
spirit: 2 oz light rum
top: A splash of club soda
method: Muddle the mint, sugar and juice; add ice, rum and soda; stir
glass: Highball
garnish: A mint sprig and a lime
```
native: Mojito = Mojito (Spanish)

## Caipirinha
![Caipirinha](commons:Caipirinha with lime.jpg)
related: Brazil
- A Caipirinha
```recipe
sour: 1 lime, cut in 8 wedges
sweet: 1 tsp sugar
spirit: 2 oz cachaça
method: Muddle the lime and sugar; add ice and cachaça; stir
glass: Lowball
garnish: Lime wedges
```
native: Caipirinha = Caipirinha (Portuguese)
> Brazil's national cocktail. Cachaça is distilled from fresh sugarcane juice.

title: Cream, frozen and coffee drinks
---
Cream drinks are shaken and poured, not strained, over ice, about 1 oz cream each. Frozen drinks are blended with
crushed ice; ice-cream drinks with no ice at all. Coffee drinks go in a mug warmed with hot water first.

## Technique
- Cream drinks are shaken, then {{poured, not strained|how?}} into a lowball glass with ice.
- Frozen drinks are blended with {{crushed ice|what kind of ice?}}, about 3 to 4 oz, at low speed for 15 to 20 seconds.
- Ice-cream drinks are blended with {{no ice|how much ice?}} and served in a Collins glass.
- Before a coffee drink, warm the mug with {{hot water|what?}} for 30 seconds, then pour it out.
- A coffee drink is topped with {{whipped cream|what?}}, a cherry and cinnamon.

## White Russian
- A White Russian
```recipe
spirit: 1 shot vodka
liqueur: 1 shot Kahlúa
cream: About 1 oz cream
method: Shaken, poured over ice
glass: Lowball
```

## Brandy Alexander
![A Brandy Alexander](commons:Brandy alexander.jpg)
- A Brandy Alexander (after its shot of brandy)
```recipe
liqueur: 1 shot dark crème de cacao
cream: About 1 oz cream
method: Shaken, poured
glass: Lowball
garnish: A sprinkle of nutmeg
```
> Made with gin and white crème de cacao it's an Alexander; with gin and white crème de menthe, Alexander's Sister.

## Grasshopper
![A Grasshopper](commons:Grasshopper cocktail.jpg)
- A Grasshopper
```recipe
liqueur: 1 shot green crème de menthe
liqueur: 1 shot white crème de cacao
cream: About 1 oz cream
method: Shaken, poured
glass: Lowball
```
> With Galliano in place of the crème de menthe, a Golden Cadillac.

## Toasted Almond and Mudslide
- A Toasted Almond is a shot each of {{amaretto|which liqueur?}} and Kahlúa, with cream.
- A Mudslide is a shot each of vodka, Kahlúa and {{Baileys Irish Cream|which liqueur?}}.
> For a Mudslide use a good vodka: a harsh one can curdle the Baileys.

## Pink Lady
![A Pink Lady](commons:Pink Lady with a twist of lime, in a cocktail glass.jpg)
- A Pink Lady
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz gin
sour: 1 oz lemon juice
sweet: 2 tbsp bar sugar and a dash of grenadine
cream: About 1 oz cream
method: Shaken, poured
glass: Lowball
```
> Without the grenadine, it's a White Lady (in the guide's version, with cream).

## Piña Colada
![A Piña Colada](commons:Piña Colada.jpg)
related: Puerto Rico, San Juan
- A Piña Colada
```recipe
spirit: 1 shot light rum
juice: 4 oz unsweetened pineapple juice
cream: 1 oz cream of coconut
other: 3 to 4 oz crushed ice
method: Blended at low for 15 to 20 seconds
glass: Hurricane (or any tall glass)
garnish: Pineapple
```
native: Piña colada = piña colada (Spanish)
> Puerto Rico's national drink since 1978.

## Frozen Margarita
- A frozen Margarita is 2 oz tequila, ½ oz triple sec and 1 oz lime juice, {{blended with crushed ice|how?}}, in a salted glass.
- Add {{2 oz fruit|how much?}} (strawberry, raspberry, peach) to make a fruit Margarita.

## Irish Coffee
![An Irish Coffee](commons:Irish coffee glass.jpg)
related: Ireland, Shannon Airport
- An Irish Coffee is a shot of {{Jameson|which whiskey?}} and a teaspoon of sugar in hot coffee, topped with cream.
> First served at Foynes, near Shannon, in the 1940s, to passengers off a cold transatlantic flight.

## Coffee drinks by country
- A Mexican Coffee is a shot of {{Kahlúa|which liqueur?}} and ½ oz tequila in coffee.
- An Italian Coffee is a shot of {{amaretto|which liqueur?}} and ½ oz Sambuca in coffee.
- A Jamaican Coffee is a shot of {{Tia Maria|which liqueur?}} and ½ oz dark rum in coffee.

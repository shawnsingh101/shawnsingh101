title: Sours, Collinses and shaken drinks
---
Shaken with ice in the mixing glass, then strained over fresh ice. Most share one shape: a spirit, something sour
(lemon or lime) and something sweet. Learn the shape and the rest are swaps.

## The sour shape
- A sour is a spirit, {{lemon juice|what sour?}} and {{sugar|what sweet?}}, shaken.
- A Collins is a sour topped with {{club soda|what?}} in a tall glass.
- Sour mix and Collins mix stand in for the juice and sugar; never {{shake|what?}} a drink made with Collins mix (it's fizzy).

## Whiskey Sour
![A Whiskey Sour](commons:Whiskey Sour.jpg)
- A Whiskey Sour
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz bourbon
sour: ½ oz lemon juice
sweet: 1 tsp bar sugar
method: Shaken, strained over ice
glass: Highball (or a sour glass)
garnish: A cherry and an orange
```
> Swap the spirit for a Scotch, Brandy or Vodka Sour. A New York Sour floats dry red wine on top; a Ward Eight adds a
> dash of grenadine.

## Amaretto Sour
![An Amaretto Sour](commons:VTR amaretto sour.jpg)
- An Amaretto Sour is 2 oz amaretto and ½ oz {{lime juice|which juice?}}, with no sugar: the liqueur is sweet enough.

## Tom Collins
![A Tom Collins](commons:VTR - Tom Collins.jpg)
- A Tom Collins
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz gin
sour: ½ oz lemon juice
sweet: 1 tsp sugar
top: A splash of club soda
method: Shaken, strained, then topped with soda
glass: Collins
garnish: A cherry
```
> With bourbon, it's a Colonel Collins; with bourbon and ginger ale in place of soda, a John Collins.

## French 75
![A French 75](commons:French 75.jpg)
related: France, Paris
- A French 75
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz cognac
sour: ½ oz lemon juice
sweet: 1 tsp sugar
wine: 4 oz Champagne
method: Shaken and strained, then topped with Champagne
glass: Collins (or a flute)
garnish: A lemon twist
```
> Named for the French 75 mm field gun of World War I. Many bars make it with gin.

## Margarita
![A Margarita](commons:MargaritaReal.jpg)
related: Mexico
- A Margarita
```recipe
spirit: 1½ oz tequila
liqueur: ½ oz triple sec
sour: 1 oz lime juice
method: Shaken, strained
glass: Lowball, salted rim
garnish: A lime
```
native: Margarita = Margarita (Spanish)

## Daiquiri
![A Daiquiri](commons:Classic Daiquiri in Cocktail Glass.jpg)
related: Cuba
- A Daiquiri
```recipe
spirit: 1 shot light rum
sour: ½ oz lime juice
sweet: 1 tsp sugar
method: Shaken, strained
glass: Lowball, rim frosted (optional)
garnish: A lime
```
native: Daiquirí = Daiquirí (Spanish)
> Named for a beach and iron mine near Santiago de Cuba.

## Kamikaze
![A Kamikaze](commons:Kamikaze-cocktail.jpg)
- A Kamikaze
```recipe
spirit: 1 shot vodka
liqueur: 1 oz triple sec
sour: ½ oz Rose's lime juice
method: Shaken, strained
glass: Lowball
garnish: A lime
```
> With blue curaçao, a Blue Kamikaze. As a shooter: vodka, triple sec and lime, equal parts.

## Mai Tai
![A Mai Tai](commons:Mai Tai at Trader Vic's Emeryville.jpg)
related: Oakland, California
- A Mai Tai
```recipe
spirit: 1 oz light rum
spirit: 1 oz dark rum
liqueur: ½ oz triple sec
sweet: ½ oz orgeat (almond syrup)
sour: ½ oz Rose's lime juice
method: Shaken, strained over ice
glass: Highball
garnish: A cherry
```
> A tiki classic from Trader Vic's in Oakland, 1944. "Maita'i" is Tahitian for "good".

## Zombie
![A Zombie](commons:ZombieCocktail.jpg)
- A Zombie
```recipe
spirit: 1 shot light rum and ½ oz dark rum
liqueur: ½ oz apricot brandy
sour: ½ oz Rose's lime juice
juice: Orange and pineapple juice, equal parts
spirit: Float ½ oz 151-proof rum
method: Shaken and strained, then the strong rum floated on top
glass: Highball
garnish: Fruit
```

## Planter's Punch
![Planter's Punch](commons:Planters Punch 2.jpg)
related: Jamaica
- A Planter's Punch
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz dark rum
liqueur: ½ oz triple sec
sour: 1 oz lime juice
sweet: 2 tsp sugar and ¼ tsp grenadine
juice: Fill with orange juice
method: Shaken, strained over ice
glass: Highball
garnish: An orange, a cherry and a sprinkle of nutmeg
```

## Singapore Sling
![A Singapore Sling at the Raffles Hotel](commons:Singapore Sling and peanuts at the Raffles Hotel Long Bar in Singapore.jpg)
related: Singapore
- A Singapore Sling
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz gin
sour: 1 oz lemon juice
sweet: 1 tsp sugar
top: A splash of club soda
liqueur: Float 1 oz cherry brandy
method: Shaken and strained, soda added, cherry brandy floated on top
glass: Highball
garnish: An orange and a cherry
```
> First mixed at the Raffles Hotel's Long Bar, Singapore, about 1915.

Served "up": chilled with ice, strained into a chilled cocktail glass, no ice in the glass. The guide's house style is to
shake them; the classic way, still preferred by many bars, is to stir anything made only of spirits.

## Martini technique
- Chill the Martini glass first by filling it with {{ice and cold water|what?}} while you mix.
- The drier the Martini, the less {{vermouth|what?}}.
- For a super-dry "in and out" Martini, swirl the vermouth in the mixing glass, then {{pour it out|what then?}}.
- Hold a chilled glass by its {{stem|where?}}, so your hand doesn't warm the drink.

## Martini
![A Martini](commons:15-09-26-RalfR-WLC-0084.jpg)
- A classic Martini
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz gin
vermouth: 1 tsp dry vermouth
method: Shaken (or stirred) with ice, strained
glass: Chilled Martini glass
garnish: An odd number of olives, or a lemon twist
```
> A Dry Martini takes ¾ tsp vermouth; an Extra Dry, ½ tsp. Many bars today pour far more: ½ oz or so.

## Sweet, Perfect and Dirty
- A Martini made with sweet vermouth is a {{Sweet Martini|which Martini?}}, garnished with a cherry.
- A Martini with equal parts dry and sweet vermouth (½ tsp each) is a {{Perfect Martini|which Martini?}}.
- A Martini with 2 tsp of olive brine is a {{Dirty Martini|which Martini?}}.
- A Martini garnished with a pearl onion is a {{Gibson|which drink?}}.

## Manhattan
![A Manhattan](commons:A Manhattan.jpg)
related: New York City
- A Manhattan
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz whiskey (rye or bourbon)
vermouth: 1 tsp sweet vermouth
bitters: A dash of Angostura bitters
method: Shaken (or stirred) with ice, strained
glass: Chilled Martini glass
garnish: A cherry
```
> The guide's spec is very dry; the common modern pour is 2 oz whiskey to 1 oz sweet vermouth. A Perfect Manhattan
> splits the vermouth, half dry, half sweet.

## Rob Roy
![A Rob Roy](commons:15-09-26-RalfR-WLC-0266.jpg)
related: Scotland
- A Rob Roy
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz scotch
vermouth: 1 tsp sweet vermouth
bitters: A few drops of Angostura bitters
method: Shaken (or stirred) with ice, strained
glass: Chilled Martini glass
garnish: A cherry
```
> A Manhattan made with scotch.

## Cosmopolitan
![A Cosmopolitan](commons:Cosmopolitan (5076906532).jpg)
- A Cosmopolitan
```recipe
spirit: 1½ oz citrus vodka
liqueur: ½ oz triple sec (Cointreau)
sour: ½ oz Rose's lime juice
juice: Cranberry juice, to color and fill
method: Shaken, strained
glass: Martini glass, rim frosted (optional)
garnish: A lime twist
```

## Gimlet
![A Gimlet](commons:Gimlet cocktail.jpg)
- A Gimlet
```recipe
spirit: 2 oz gin
sour: ½ oz Rose's lime juice
method: Shaken, strained
glass: Martini glass, rim frosted (optional)

```

## Sidecar

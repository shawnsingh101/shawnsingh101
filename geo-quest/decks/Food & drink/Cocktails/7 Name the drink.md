The other way around: given what's in it, which cocktail is it?

## Which cocktail?
- Gin, Campari and sweet vermouth in equal parts, stirred over ice, with an orange peel: a {{Negroni|which cocktail?}}.
- Whiskey, sweet vermouth and Angostura bitters, served up with a cherry: a {{Manhattan|which cocktail?}}.
- Scotch, sweet vermouth and bitters, served up with a cherry: a {{Rob Roy|which cocktail?}}.
- Bourbon, sugar muddled with bitters and soda, in a lowball with a cherry and orange: an {{Old Fashioned|which cocktail?}}.
- Gin and a whisper of dry vermouth with a pearl onion: a {{Gibson|which cocktail?}}.
- Gin and Rose's lime juice, shaken, served up: a {{Gimlet|which cocktail?}}.
- Citrus vodka, triple sec, lime and cranberry, served up with a lime twist: a {{Cosmopolitan|which cocktail?}}.
- Cognac, Cointreau and lemon juice, shaken, sometimes a sugared rim: a {{Sidecar|which cocktail?}}.
- Tequila, triple sec and lime juice in a salted glass: a {{Margarita|which cocktail?}}.
- Light rum, lime juice and sugar, shaken: a {{Daiquiri|which cocktail?}}.
- Rum, mint, sugar, lime and a splash of soda: a {{Mojito|which cocktail?}}.
- Cachaça, muddled lime and sugar: a {{Caipirinha|which cocktail?}}.
- Bourbon, muddled mint, sugar and water, in a silver cup: a {{Mint Julep|which cocktail?}}.
- Gin, lemon, sugar and club soda in a tall glass: a {{Tom Collins|which cocktail?}}.
- Cognac, lemon and sugar, topped with Champagne: a {{French 75|which cocktail?}}.
- Light and dark rum, triple sec, orgeat and lime: a {{Mai Tai|which cocktail?}}.
- Gin, lemon, sugar and soda with a cherry-brandy float: a {{Singapore Sling|which cocktail?}}.
- Vodka and orange juice with a Galliano float: a {{Harvey Wallbanger|which cocktail?}}.
- Vodka, lime and cranberry juice: a {{Cape Codder|which cocktail?}}.
- Vodka and grapefruit juice in a salted glass: a {{Salty Dog|which cocktail?}}.
- Vodka, grapefruit and a splash of cranberry: a {{Sea Breeze|which cocktail?}}.
- Vodka and Kahlúa on the rocks: a {{Black Russian|which cocktail?}}.
- Vodka, Kahlúa and cream: a {{White Russian|which cocktail?}}.
- Scotch and Drambuie on the rocks: a {{Rusty Nail|which cocktail?}}.
- Scotch and amaretto on the rocks: a {{Godfather|which cocktail?}}.
- Brandy and white crème de menthe on the rocks: a {{Stinger|which cocktail?}}.
- A shot of cognac, dark crème de cacao and cream with nutmeg: a {{Brandy Alexander|which cocktail?}}.
- Green crème de menthe, white crème de cacao and cream: a {{Grasshopper|which cocktail?}}.
- Rum, pineapple juice and cream of coconut, blended: a {{Piña Colada|which cocktail?}}.
- Kahlúa, Baileys and Grand Marnier, layered: a {{B-52|which shot?}}.

Example deck: holy sites of five religions, linked to the places on the map with `related:` lines.

## Kaaba
related: Mecca, Saudi Arabia, Masjid al-Haram
- The Kaaba stands in the Masjid al-Haram in {{Mecca}}, Saudi Arabia.
- The direction Muslims face in prayer, towards the Kaaba :: the qibla

## Golden Temple
related: Amritsar, Punjab, India
- The Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib) is the holiest gurdwara of {{Sikhism}}.
- The Golden Temple is in the city of {{Amritsar}}, in the Indian state of Punjab.

## Western Wall
related: Jerusalem, Old City of Jerusalem
- The Western Wall in {{Jerusalem}} is the holiest place where Jews are permitted to pray.

## St. Peter's Basilica
related: Vatican City, Rome
- St. Peter's Basilica stands in {{Vatican City}}, the smallest country in the world.

## Varanasi
related: Ganges, Uttar Pradesh, India
- Varanasi, on the {{Ganges}}, is one of Hinduism's holiest cities.

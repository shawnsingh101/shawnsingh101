## Song dynasty
- The Song dynasty ruled from {{c1::960|when?}} to {{c1::1279|when?}}.
- The Northern Song ruled from {{Kaifeng|what city?}}.
![Kaifeng](commons:鼓楼夜市 - panoramio (1).jpg)
native: 开封 Kāifēng (Chinese)
- After 1127 the Southern Song ruled from {{Hangzhou|what city?}}.
![Hangzhou](commons:Huanglong & Broken Bridge - Hangzhou City & West Lake near Broken Bridge.jpg)
native: 杭州 Hángzhōu (Chinese)
- Movable type printing was invented under the Song by {{Bi Sheng|whom?}}.
![The Dream Pool Essays, which describe Bi Sheng's movable type](commons:Mengxi bitan.jpg)
native: 毕昇 Bì Shēng (Chinese)
- The Song were the first government to issue {{paper money|what?}}.
![Jiaozi, early Song paper money](commons:Jiao zi.jpg)
native: 交子 jiāozǐ (Chinese)
> The Liao (Khitan), Jin (Jurchen) and Western Xia (Tangut) ruled the north beside the Song.

## Song dynasty
native: 宋朝 Sòng Cháo (Chinese)
- The Song dynasty ruled from {{960|when?}} to {{1279|when?}}.
- The Northern Song ruled from {{Kaifeng|what city?}}; after 1127 the Southern Song ruled from {{Hangzhou|what city?}}.
- Movable type printing was invented under the Song by {{Bi Sheng|whom?}}.
- The Song were the first government to issue {{paper money|what?}}.
>The Liao (Khitan), Jin (Jurchen) and Western Xia (Tangut) ruled the north beside the Song.

## Which dynasty?
- The dynasty of movable type, paper money and the magnetic compass at sea :: the Song dynasty

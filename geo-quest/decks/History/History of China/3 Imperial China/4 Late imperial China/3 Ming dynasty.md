## Ming dynasty
- The Ming dynasty ruled from {{c1::1368|when?}} to {{c1::1644|when?}}.
- The Ming was founded by {{Zhu Yuanzhang|whom?}}, the Hongwu Emperor, who drove out the Mongols.
![Zhu Yuanzhang, the Hongwu Emperor](commons:Portrait assis de l'empereur Ming Taizu.jpg)
native: 朱元璋 Zhū Yuánzhāng (Chinese)
- The {{Forbidden City|what?}} in Beijing was built under the Ming (1406–1420).
![The Forbidden City](commons:Hall of Supreme Harmony (20241127120000).jpg)
native: 紫禁城 Zǐjìnchéng (Chinese)
- Admiral {{Zheng He|who?}} led seven great voyages to Africa and Arabia (1405–1433).
![Zheng He](commons:Zheng He.jpg)
native: 郑和 Zhèng Hé (Chinese)
- Most of the {{Great Wall|what?}} you can visit today was rebuilt in brick by the Ming.

native: 长城 Chángchéng (Chinese)

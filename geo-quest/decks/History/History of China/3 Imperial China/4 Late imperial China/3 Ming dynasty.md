## Ming dynasty
native: 明朝 Míng Cháo (Chinese)
- The Ming dynasty ruled from {{1368|when?}} to {{1644|when?}}.
- The Ming was founded by {{Zhu Yuanzhang|whom?}}, the Hongwu Emperor, who drove out the Mongols.
- The {{Forbidden City|what?}} in Beijing was built under the Ming (1406–1420).
- Admiral {{Zheng He|who?}} led seven great voyages to Africa and Arabia (1405–1433).
- Most of the Great Wall you can visit today was rebuilt in {{brick|what?}} by the Ming.

## Which dynasty?
- The dynasty that built the Forbidden City and rebuilt the Great Wall :: the Ming dynasty

## In order
- The dynasty or period after the Yuan dynasty :: the Ming dynasty

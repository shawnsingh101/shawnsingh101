## Yuan dynasty
native: 元朝 Yuán Cháo (Chinese)
- The Yuan dynasty ruled from {{1271|when?}} to {{1368|when?}}.
- The Yuan was founded by the Mongol ruler {{Kublai Khan|who?}}, grandson of Genghis Khan.
- The Yuan capital, Dadu, is today's {{Beijing|what city?}}.
- The Venetian traveller {{Marco Polo|who?}} described Kublai Khan's court.

## Which dynasty?
- The Mongol dynasty of Kublai Khan :: the Yuan dynasty

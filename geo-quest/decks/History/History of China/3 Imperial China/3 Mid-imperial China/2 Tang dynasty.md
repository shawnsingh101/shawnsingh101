## Tang dynasty
native: 唐朝 Táng Cháo (Chinese)
- The Tang dynasty ruled from {{618|when?}} to {{907|when?}}.
- The Tang capital, {{Chang'an|what city?}}, was the largest city in the world.
- {{Wu Zetian|who?}} was the only woman to rule China as emperor in her own name.
- The poets {{Li Bai|who?}} and {{Du Fu|who?}} wrote under the Tang.
- The oldest dated printed book, the Diamond Sutra (868), was printed by {{woodblock|which method?}} under the Tang.

## Tang dynasty
- The Tang dynasty ruled from {{c1::618|when?}} to {{c1::907|when?}}.
- The Tang capital, {{Chang'an|what city?}}, was the largest city in the world.
![Chang'an (today's Xi'an)](commons:ChinaTrip2005-110.jpg)
native: 长安 Cháng'ān (Chinese)
- {{Wu Zetian|who?}} was the only woman to rule China as emperor in her own name.
![Wu Zetian](commons:唐后行從圖 (局部) - 武則天.png)
native: 武则天 Wǔ Zétiān (Chinese)
- {{Li Bai|who?}}, with Du Fu, is one of China's two greatest poets, both of the Tang.
![Li Bai](commons:李白.png)
native: 李白 Lǐ Bái (Chinese)
- The oldest dated printed book, the Diamond Sutra (868), was printed by {{woodblock|which method?}} under the Tang.
![The Diamond Sutra](commons:Jingangjing.jpg)
native: 雕版印刷 diāobǎn yìnshuā (Chinese)

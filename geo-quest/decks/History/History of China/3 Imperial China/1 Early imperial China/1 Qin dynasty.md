## Qin dynasty
native: 秦朝 Qín Cháo (Chinese)
- The Qin dynasty ruled from {{221 BC|when?}} to {{206 BC|when?}}.
- The Qin's founder, {{Qin Shi Huang|who?}}, was the first emperor of a unified China.
- Qin Shi Huang standardised {{writing|what?}}, weights, measures and coins across China.
- Qin Shi Huang was buried with the {{Terracotta Army|what?}} near today's Xi'an.
>The name "China" may come from Qin.

## Which dynasty?
- The short dynasty that first unified China :: the Qin dynasty

## In order
- The dynasty or period after the Warring States period :: the Qin dynasty

## Qin dynasty
![The Qin empire in 210 BC](commons:Qin empire 210 BCE.png)
- The Qin dynasty ruled from {{221 BC|when?}} to {{206 BC|when?}}.
- The Qin's founder, {{Qin Shi Huang|who?}}, was the first emperor of a unified China.
> The name "China" may come from Qin.
- Qin Shi Huang made one {{writing|what?}} system, and one set of weights, measures and coins, for all of China.
- Qin Shi Huang was buried with the {{Terracotta Army|what?}} near today's Xi'an.

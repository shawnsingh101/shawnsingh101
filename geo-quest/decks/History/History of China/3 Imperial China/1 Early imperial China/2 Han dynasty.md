## Han dynasty
- The Han dynasty ruled from {{c1::206 BC|when?}} to {{c1::AD 220|when?}}.
- The Han was founded by {{Liu Bang|whom?}}.
![Liu Bang, Emperor Gaozu of Han](commons:Liu-bang.jpg)
native: 刘邦 Liú Bāng (Chinese)
- The Silk Road to Central Asia opened under the Han, after the journeys of {{Zhang Qian|whom?}}.
![Zhang Qian sets out for the west](commons:ZhangQianTravels.jpg)
native: 张骞 Zhāng Qiān (Chinese)
- Paper was improved and made practical under the Han by {{Cai Lun|whom?}}, about AD 105.
![Cai Lun](commons:Cai-lun.jpg)
native: 蔡伦 Cài Lún (Chinese)
- The Western Han ruled from {{Chang'an|what city?}}.
![Chang'an (today's Xi'an)](commons:ChinaTrip2005-110.jpg)
native: 长安 Cháng'ān (Chinese)
- The Eastern Han ruled from {{Luoyang|what city?}}.
native: 洛阳 Luòyáng (Chinese)
> Wang Mang's Xin dynasty (AD 9–23) split the Han into Western and Eastern. Most Chinese people still call themselves Han.

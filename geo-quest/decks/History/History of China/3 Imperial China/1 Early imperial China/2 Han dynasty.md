## Han dynasty
native: 汉朝 Hàn Cháo (Chinese)
- The Han dynasty ruled from {{206 BC|when?}} to {{AD 220|when?}}.
- The Han was founded by {{Liu Bang|whom?}}.
- The Silk Road to Central Asia opened under the Han, after the journeys of {{Zhang Qian|whom?}}.
- Paper was improved and made practical by {{Cai Lun|whom?}} under the Han, about AD 105.
- The Western Han ruled from {{Chang'an|what city?}}; the Eastern Han from {{Luoyang|what city?}}.
>Most Chinese people still call themselves Han after this dynasty. Wang Mang's Xin dynasty (AD 9–23) split it into Western and Eastern Han.

## Which dynasty?
- The dynasty of the Silk Road, which gave its name to the Han Chinese :: the Han dynasty

## In order

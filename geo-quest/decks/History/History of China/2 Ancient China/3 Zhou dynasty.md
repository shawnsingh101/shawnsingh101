## Zhou dynasty
- The Zhou dynasty ruled from {{c1::1046 BC|when?}} to {{c1::256 BC|when?}}.
- The Zhou ruled longer than any other dynasty: about {{790|how many?}} years.
- The Zhou claimed the {{Mandate of Heaven|what?}}: Heaven gives the right to rule to a just king and takes it from a bad one.
native: 天命 Tiānmìng (Chinese)
- The Western Zhou capital was near today's {{Xi'an|what city?}}.
native: 西安 Xī'ān (Chinese)
- After 771 BC, the Eastern Zhou ruled from {{Luoyang|what city?}}.
![Luoyang](commons:China Luoyang.png)
native: 洛阳 Luòyáng (Chinese)

## Spring and Autumn period
- The Spring and Autumn period ran from {{c1::722 BC|when?}} to {{c1::476 BC|when?}}.
- {{Confucius|who?}} taught in the Spring and Autumn period.
![Confucius](commons:Confucius, fresco from a Western Han tomb of Dongping County, Shandong province, China.jpg)
native: 孔子 Kǒngzǐ (Chinese)
> Confucius lived from 551 to 479 BC.
- The Spring and Autumn period is named after a chronicle of the state of {{Lu|which state?}}.
![The Spring and Autumn Annals](commons:Spring and Autumn Annals annoted.JPG)
native: 鲁 Lǔ (Chinese)

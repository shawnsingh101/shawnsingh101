## Zhou dynasty
native: 周朝 Zhōu Cháo (Chinese)
- The Zhou dynasty ruled from {{1046 BC|when?}} to {{256 BC|when?}}.
- The Zhou ruled longer than any other dynasty: about {{790|how many?}} years.
- The Zhou claimed the {{Mandate of Heaven|what?}}: Heaven gives the right to rule to a just king and takes it away from a bad one.
- The Western Zhou capital was near today's {{Xi'an|what city?}}; after 771 BC the Eastern Zhou ruled from {{Luoyang|what city?}}.

## Which dynasty?
- The longest-ruling dynasty, which claimed the Mandate of Heaven :: the Zhou dynasty

## In order
- The dynasty or period after the Shang dynasty :: the Zhou dynasty

## Spring and Autumn period
native: 春秋 Chūnqiū (Chinese)
- The Spring and Autumn period ran from {{722 BC|when?}} to {{476 BC|when?}}.
- {{Confucius|who?}} (551–479 BC) taught in the Spring and Autumn period.
- The period is named after the Spring and Autumn {{Annals|what?}}, a chronicle of the state of Lu.

## Warring States period
native: 战国 Zhànguó (Chinese)
- In the Warring States period (476–221 BC), {{seven|how many?}} big states fought for control.
- The Art of War is credited to {{Sun Tzu|whom?}}.
- The Warring States period ended when {{Qin|which state?}} conquered the others in 221 BC.

## Paleolithic China
- The earliest known human relatives in China, {{Yuanmou Man|who?}}, lived about 1.7 million years ago.
![Teeth of Yuanmou Man (cast)](commons:Teeth of Yuanmou Man (Cast) - cropped.png)
native: 元谋人 Yuánmóu Rén (Chinese)
- Peking Man, a Homo erectus, was found at {{Zhoukoudian|where?}} near Beijing.
![Peking Man (a reconstruction)](commons:Sinathropus pekinensis.jpg)
native: 周口店 Zhōukǒudiàn (Chinese)
> Peking Man lived roughly 750,000 to 400,000 years ago.
related: Peking Man Site at Zhoukoudian, Beijing

## Properties of life
- All living things share nine properties: {{c1::order}}, {{c1::sensitivity}}, {{c1::reproduction}}, {{c2::adaptation}}, {{c2::growth and development}}, {{c2::regulation}}, {{c3::homeostasis}}, {{c3::energy processing}} and {{c3::evolution}}.
> One card per three properties; the other six are shown as clues.
- Living things are highly organized, coordinated structures, a property called {{order|which property?}}.
![A toad: a highly organized structure of cells, tissues and organs · OpenStax Biology 2e (CC BY 4.0)](https://openstax.org/apps/image-cdn/v1/f=webp/apps/archive/20260604.144757/resources/cfb551769031704de85fc03c9f472548764ed41c)
- Responding to the environment, like the leaves of the "sensitive plant" folding when touched, is called {{sensitivity|which property?}}.
![The sensitive plant, Mimosa pudica, folds its leaves when touched · OpenStax Biology 2e (CC BY 4.0)](https://openstax.org/apps/image-cdn/v1/f=webp/apps/archive/20260604.144757/resources/b4d91acb1aec2baf52e9877fd4975016051161a8)
species: Mimosa pudica
- Living things pass their DNA, and so their traits, to offspring through {{reproduction|which property?}}.
![Kittens inherit genes from both parents · OpenStax Biology 2e (CC BY 4.0)](https://openstax.org/apps/image-cdn/v1/f=webp/apps/archive/20260604.144757/resources/e36312479caf0cf78561bf6fd145690e070b5ade)
- Keeping a stable internal environment, such as body temperature, is called {{homeostasis|which property?}}.
![A polar bear keeps its body warm in the cold · OpenStax Biology 2e (CC BY 4.0)](https://openstax.org/apps/image-cdn/v1/f=webp/apps/archive/20260604.144757/resources/f36db18e3d340be14ce830ec3f43468678895e0f)
- Using energy from food or sunlight to power life's work is called {{energy processing|which property?}}.
![A California condor uses energy from food to fly · OpenStax Biology 2e (CC BY 4.0)](https://openstax.org/apps/image-cdn/v1/f=webp/apps/archive/20260604.144757/resources/bf69acd5fab5ca9a827c175f7d289b7be6d3dec8)

## Levels of organization
- Life is organized from small to large: {{c1::atom}}, {{c1::molecule}}, {{c1::organelle}}, {{c1::cell}}, {{c2::tissue}}, {{c2::organ}}, {{c2::organ system}}, {{c2::organism}}, {{c3::population}}, {{c3::community}}, {{c3::ecosystem}}, {{c3::biosphere}}.
![The levels of organization of living things · OpenStax Biology 2e (CC BY 4.0)](https://openstax.org/apps/image-cdn/v1/f=webp/apps/archive/20260604.144757/resources/0efa3270cbc7ea6e9dac481b9ca2523ec08b068f)
- All the individuals of one species living in one area make up a {{population|which level?}}.

## The three domains of life
- All life falls into three domains: {{c1::Bacteria}}, {{c1::Archaea}} and {{c1::Eukarya}}.

## How science works
- Reasoning from many specific observations to a general conclusion is {{inductive|which kind?}} reasoning.

![Inductive and deductive reasoning · OpenStax Biology 2e (CC BY 4.0)](https://openstax.org/apps/image-cdn/v1/f=webp/apps/archive/20260604.144757/resources/74d85631b95b42ac995c6271adb22206df437606)

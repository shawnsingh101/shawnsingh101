## The study of life
- Biology is the scientific study of {{life|what?}}.
- The smallest unit that shows all the properties of life :: the cell
- The three domains of life :: Bacteria, Archaea and Eukarya

## Properties of life
- Keeping a stable internal environment, such as body temperature, is called {{homeostasis|what?}}.
- The properties shared by all living things :: order, sensitivity to the environment, reproduction, adaptation, growth and development, regulation, homeostasis, energy processing, and evolution

## Levels of organization
- Living things are organized from small to large: atom, molecule, organelle, cell, {{tissue|what?}}, organ, organ system, organism, population, community, ecosystem, biosphere.

## How science works
- Reasoning from many specific observations to a general conclusion :: inductive reasoning
- Reasoning from a general principle to predict a specific result :: deductive reasoning

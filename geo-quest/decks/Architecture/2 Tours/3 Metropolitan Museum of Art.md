## The museum
related: New York City, Metropolitan Museum of Art
- The Met was founded in 1870; its first president was {{John Taylor Johnston|who?}}, a railroad executive and collector.
![John Taylor Johnston](commons:John Taylor Johnston cph.3a03196.jpg)
- In 1880 the museum moved to its present site, in {{Central Park|which park?}} on Fifth Avenue.
![Central Park](commons:Southwest corner of Central Park, looking east, NYC.jpg)
- Its first building there, in red-brick Gothic Revival, was designed by {{Calvert Vaux|who?}}, co-designer of Central Park.
![Calvert Vaux](commons:CVaux.jpg)
- The grand Fifth Avenue front, opened in 1902, was designed by {{Richard Morris Hunt|who?}}.
![Richard Morris Hunt](commons:Richard Morris Hunt.jpg)
- The Fifth Avenue front is in the {{Beaux-Arts|which style?}} style.
native: Beaux-Arts (French)
![The Met's Fifth Avenue front](commons:Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Met) - Central Park, NYC.jpg)
- From 1967, the glass-walled wings and the museum's master plan were designed by {{Kevin Roche|who?}} and John Dinkeloo.
![Kevin Roche](commons:Kevin Roche krb.00028 (cropped).jpg)

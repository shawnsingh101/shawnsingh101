Karen Hao, Empire of AI: Dreams and Nightmares in Sam Altman's OpenAI (2025). Based on over 300 interviews with about 260 people, including more than 90 current and former OpenAI staff.
The argument: the companies racing to build AI behave like empires. They take what isn't theirs (writers' and artists' work, people's data, land, energy and water), rely on cheap labor around the world to clean and label data, and justify it all with a promise of progress and a race against rival empires. OpenAI, founded to keep AI open and safe, became the model for this.
The book: a prologue on Altman's firing in November 2023; Part I, the founding and the bet on scale; Part II, Microsoft, secrecy and commerce; Part III, ChatGPT, its human and environmental costs, and the safety camp's revolt; Part IV, the firing and what followed; an epilogue on another way to build AI (Te Hiku Media, Timnit Gebru's DAIR).
Key terms: AGI · nonprofit and capped-profit · the Transformer · scaling laws · RLHF · data annotation · Doomers and Boomers · effective altruism.
Key people: Sam Altman · Elon Musk · Greg Brockman · Ilya Sutskever · Mira Murati · Dario Amodei · Timnit Gebru.

## Empire of AI: how it works
- In 2017 Google invented the {{Transformer|what?}}, a kind of neural network that reads each word in a long context: the "T" in GPT.
also in: How AI works, History of AI
- {{Scaling laws|what?}}: the finding, by Dario Amodei's team at OpenAI, that models improve predictably with more data, computing power and size. Hao argues it became a self-fulfilling strategy.
also in: How AI works
- ChatGPT learned to follow instructions through {{reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF)|what technique?}}: people rate its answers, over and over.
also in: How AI works

## Empire of AI: the industry and its costs
- In 2021 a group led by {{Dario Amodei|whom?}} left OpenAI over safety (and power), a split insiders called "The Divorce", and founded Anthropic.
also in: The AI industry, History of AI
![Dario Amodei](commons:Dario Amodei at TechCrunch Disrupt 2023 02 (cropped).jpg)
- OpenAI paid Kenyan workers at the outsourcing firm {{Sama|which firm?}} less than $2 an hour to filter violent and abusive content out of its models.
also in: The AI industry, AI debates
- The industry's data centers are spreading abroad too: in {{Chile|which South American country?}}, activists found that a planned Google data center would draw about 169 liters of drinking water a second.
also in: The AI industry, AI debates
![The Atacama Desert, Chile](commons:Atacama.png)
- Microsoft and OpenAI were planning {{Stargate|what project?}}: a single supercomputer costing around $100 billion.

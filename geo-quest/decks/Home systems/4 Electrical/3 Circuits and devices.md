From the panel to the wall: cable and conduit, wire colors, outlets (standard, GFCI and AFCI), switches, light fixtures, and the low-voltage wiring for doorbells, thermostats and data.

## How wires are run
- Most house wiring is {{nonmetallic sheathed cable|what?}} (NM cable, often called by the brand name Romex): insulated wires in a plastic jacket.
![NM cable](commons:Romex cable.agr.jpg)
- {{Conduit|what?}} is metal or plastic tubing that wires are pulled through, used where they need protection.
![Conduit on a wall](commons:Texaco nanticoke conduit galore.jpg)
- Every splice, outlet and switch must sit inside an {{electrical box|what?}} with a cover.

## Wire colors
- In US house wiring, black or red insulation marks a {{hot|which wire?}} wire.
- In US house wiring, white insulation marks the {{neutral|which wire?}} wire.
- In US house wiring, a green or bare copper wire is the {{ground|which wire?}} wire.

## Outlets
- On a standard US outlet, the shorter slot is the hot and the taller slot is the {{neutral|which wire?}}; the round hole is the ground.
- A {{GFCI (ground-fault circuit interrupter)|which device?}} shuts off power in a fraction of a second when current leaks out of the circuit, as through a person.
![A GFCI outlet](commons:Residential GFCI receptacle.jpg)
- GFCI protection is required in bathrooms, kitchens, garages, basements, outdoors, and near any {{water|what?}}.
- An {{AFCI (arc-fault circuit interrupter)|which device?}} shuts off a circuit when it senses the sparking of a damaged or loose wire, a common cause of house fires.
![An AFCI breaker](commons:Arc Fault Circuit Interrupter.jpg)
- New outlets have spring-loaded shutters that keep children from pushing things in: they are {{tamper-resistant|what kind?}} outlets.

## Switches and lights
- A plain on-off light switch with two terminals and a ground is a {{single-pole|which kind?}} switch.
![A light switch](commons:Rocker light switch.jpg)
- To control one light from two places, like the top and bottom of a stairway, use two {{three-way|which kind?}} switches.
- Between two three-way switches, any number of {{four-way|which kind?}} switches add more places to control the light.
- A {{dimmer|what?}} is a switch that varies how bright a light is.
- A ceiling light or fan is hardwired to an {{electrical box|what?}} in the ceiling; a fan's box must be rated to hold it.

## Low voltage
- A doorbell runs on low voltage from a small {{transformer|what?}} that turns 120 volts into about 16.
- Most thermostats run on {{24 volts|how much?}}, from a transformer in the furnace or air handler.
- Data cable for wired internet, like Cat 5e or Cat 6, is {{twisted pair|what kind?}} cable.
![Data cable](commons:Cat 5e cable - 1.jpg)

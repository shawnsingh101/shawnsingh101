---
title: Roof and ground drainage
---
Keeping water away from the foundation: gutters, downspouts and extensions for the roof; grading, swales, French drains and window wells for the yard; and the drain tile and sump pump that are the last line of defense.

:::lesson
## Why it matters
Most wet basements are caused not by a high water table but by **roof and surface water** that is let soak in next to the house. Saturated soil pushes on the foundation (**hydrostatic pressure**) and finds every crack. The fix is usually in order of cost: clean the gutters, extend the downspouts, regrade the soil, then add drains or a sump pump.
:::

## Roof drainage
- {{Gutters|what?}} run along the roof's edge and catch the rain running off it.
![A rain gutter](commons:UPVC Rainwater goods 2084.JPG)
- A {{downspout|what?}} carries the water from the gutter down to the ground.
![A downspout](commons:Rain gutter.jpg)
- A downspout should let the water out at least {{4 to 6 feet|how far?}} from the foundation, through an extension or underground pipe.
- A {{splash block|what?}} is a concrete or plastic trough under a downspout that steers water away from the wall.
- Gutters slope gently toward the downspouts, about {{¼ inch|how much?}} for every 10 feet.

## Grading
- The ground around a house should slope away from it, falling at least {{6 inches|how much?}} in the first 10 feet.
> That is the International Residential Code's rule; soil settles next to new foundations, so check the slope every few years.
- A {{swale|what?}} is a shallow, grassy channel that carries surface water around the house and away.
- Soil, mulch or a patio sloping toward the house is called {{negative grading|what?}}.

## Ground drainage
- A {{French drain|what?}} is a gravel-filled trench with a perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away.
![A French drain being built](commons:FrenchDrain-02.jpg)
- A {{window well|what?}} is the U-shaped liner that holds the soil back from a basement window below ground level; it needs a drain at the bottom.
![A window well](commons:Basement window-well coverings (13975399765).jpg)

## Inside the basement
- A perforated pipe laid around the inside or outside of the footing to collect water is {{drain tile|what?}}.
- Drain tile carries water to a {{sump pit|what?}}, a lined hole in the basement floor.
- A {{sump pump|what?}} pumps the water that collects in the pit up and out of the house.
![A sump pump](commons:Sump pump AC.jpg)
- A {{check valve|which valve?}} on the sump pump's discharge pipe keeps the water from running back into the pit.

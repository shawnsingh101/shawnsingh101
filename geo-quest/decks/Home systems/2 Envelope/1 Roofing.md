The building envelope is the house's skin: everything that keeps weather out. First the roof: what covers it, what lies under the covering, and the trim and vents at its edges.

## Coverings
- The most common roof covering on American houses is the {{asphalt shingle|which covering?}}.
- A metal roof whose panels are joined by raised, folded seams is a {{standing-seam|which kind?}} metal roof.
![A standing-seam metal roof](commons:Standing seam metal roof low pitch roof-2.jpg)
- Heavy, long-lasting {{clay or concrete tiles|what covering?}} are common on Spanish-style and Mediterranean houses.
![A clay tile roof](commons:Roof Tiles 14-09-06 071.jpg)

## Under the covering
- The {{roof deck|what?}}, sheets of plywood or OSB nailed over the rafters or trusses, is what the roofing is fastened to.
- {{Underlayment|which layer?}}, felt or a synthetic sheet, is laid over the roof deck under the shingles as a second line against water.
- An {{ice and water shield|what?}}, a self-sealing rubbery membrane, goes along the eaves and in valleys where ice dams back water up.

## Where water gets in
- {{Flashing|what?}} is thin metal (or membrane) that seals the joints where a roof meets a chimney, a wall, a valley or a vent pipe.
- An {{ice dam|what?}} is a ridge of ice at the eaves, formed when heat from the house melts snow higher up and it refreezes at the cold edge.

## The edges
- The {{fascia|which board?}} is the vertical board along the roof edge, where the gutter is hung.
- The {{soffit|which part?}} is the underside of the roof overhang, often vented to let air into the attic.
![Soffit and fascia](commons:View Of Soffit.jpg)
- The {{eaves|which part?}} are the lower edges of the roof that overhang the walls.

## Attic air
- A {{ridge vent|which vent?}} runs along the peak of the roof and lets warm, moist attic air out.
- In a vented attic, air comes in low at the {{soffit vents|which vents?}} and leaves high at the ridge.
> Moving air keeps the roof deck cold and dry in winter (fewer ice dams, less mold) and cooler in summer.

## Problems
- Debt is 60% of GDP, $r = 4\%$, $g = 2\%$. The primary surplus that holds the ratio steady is about {{1.2\% of GDP|how much?}}.
eq: Debt-stabilizing primary balance | pb^* = \frac{r - g}{1+g}\,b
- Same country, but growth rises to 4%: the stabilizing primary balance becomes {{zero|how much?}}.
- Debt is 100% of GDP and the rate rises 2 points with growth unchanged: the stabilizing surplus rises by about {{2\% of GDP|how much?}}.
- A country's debt is mostly short-term and in dollars, and its rate jumps: a self-fulfilling {{debt crisis|what?}} is a risk.

Exam-style practice on Lecture 2: growth accounting with numbers, GDP per worker, and which policy works through which term.

## With numbers
- $Y = 20K^{1/2}N^{1/2}$ and employment falls 2%, with $K$ and TFP unchanged: GDP falls {{1%|by how much?}}.
- In the same economy, GDP per worker {{rises 1%|what happens to it?}}, since output falls less than employment.
- GDP grows 3%, capital 4%, labor 1%, and $\alpha = 1/3$: TFP grew {{1%|how much?}}.
- GDP grows more slowly than the weighted growth of capital and labor: TFP growth was {{negative|positive or negative?}}.
- With $\alpha = 1/3$, 3% growth in capital alone adds {{1%|how much?}} to GDP growth.

## Which policy, which term?
- Strengthening the rule of law raises GDP through {{total factor productivity|which term?}}.
- Tax breaks that encourage firms to invest raise GDP per person through capital {{deepening|which term?}}.
- Helping more women into paid work raises GDP per person through labor {{utilization|which term?}}.

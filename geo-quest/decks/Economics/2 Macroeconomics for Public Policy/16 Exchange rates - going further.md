## Problems
- 100 yen per dollar, a burger at 2 dollars in the US and 250 yen in Japan: the real exchange rate is {{0.8|what?}} Japanese burgers per US burger.
eq: Real exchange rate | e = \frac{e_{nom}\,P}{P_{For}}
- In that case PPP says the yen–dollar rate should move toward {{125 yen per dollar|what?}}.
- Foreign rates are 2% and the home currency is expected to lose 3%: home rates must be {{5\%|what?}}.
eq: Uncovered interest parity | i = i_{For} - \frac{\Delta e_{nom}^{e}}{e_{nom}}
- The Fed raises rates while others hold: the dollar {{appreciates|appreciates or depreciates?}} and US net exports fall.

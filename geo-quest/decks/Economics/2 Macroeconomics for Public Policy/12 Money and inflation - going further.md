## Problems
- Money grows 10% a year, velocity is steady, output grows 3%: inflation is {{7\%|how much?}}.
eq: Quantity theory in growth rates | \pi = g_M + g_V - g_Y
- $M = 2{,}000$, $V = 5$, $Y = 8{,}000$: the price level is {{1.25|what?}}.
eq: Quantity equation | M\,V = P\,Y
- Mobile payments let people hold less cash, so velocity rises: with money and output unchanged, prices {{rise|rise or fall?}}.
- A central bank raises money growth from 2% to 6% for good: in the long run the nominal interest rate rises by about {{4 points|how much?}}.
- Inflation turns out higher than everyone expected when loans were made: wealth moves from lenders to {{borrowers|whom?}}.

The dinosaurs worth knowing, by branch of the tree. Each card shows the dinosaur, life-like and as a fossil, and asks when it lived: the period, the epoch and the age (the finest slice of the time scale), as its Wikipedia article gives it, so the cards follow new finds. Dinosaurs are told apart by their bones, so there are no “which dinosaur is this?” cards. They also appear in Earth history.

The whales and dolphins worth knowing on sight, by family. Each card shows a photo: name the animal. The back gives its scientific name (read aloud), its place in the tree of life from the top down, and where it lives, with a range map.

## Rorquals | Balaenopteridae
- Q42196 Blue whale | in every ocean, feeding in cold polar waters; the largest animal that has ever lived
- Q179020 Fin whale | in every ocean, mostly temperate and polar waters
- Q132905 Humpback whale | in every ocean, migrating between polar feeding grounds and tropical breeding grounds
- Q13612407 Minke whale | in every ocean; the smallest rorqual

## Right whales and the bowhead | Balaenidae
- Q174652 Bowhead whale | in the Arctic, among the sea ice
- Q193683 North Atlantic right whale | along the coasts of the North Atlantic; critically endangered
- Q244410 Southern right whale | in the oceans of the Southern Hemisphere

## The gray whale | Eschrichtiidae
- Q179154 Gray whale | in the North Pacific, migrating along the coast between Alaska and Mexico

## The sperm whale | Physeteridae
- Q81214 Sperm whale | in deep oceans worldwide

## Oceanic dolphins | Delphinidae
- Q26843 Orca | in every ocean, most in cold waters
- Q174199 Common bottlenose dolphin | in warm and temperate seas worldwide
- Q207114 Common dolphin | in warm and temperate seas worldwide, often in huge groups
- Q649513 Spinner dolphin | in tropical oceans
- Q207744 Long-finned pilot whale | in the cold North Atlantic and the Southern Ocean
- Q203897 Risso's dolphin | in deep waters worldwide

## Porpoises | Phocoenidae
- Q27027 Harbour porpoise | along the coasts of the North Atlantic and North Pacific
- Q725493 Vaquita | only in the northern Gulf of California; the rarest marine mammal
- Q738564 Dall's porpoise | in the North Pacific

## The narwhal and beluga | Monodontidae
- Q159426 Narwhal | in Arctic waters around Greenland and Canada
- Q132072 Beluga whale | in Arctic and sub-Arctic waters

## Beaked whales | Ziphiidae
- Q623173 Cuvier's beaked whale | in deep oceans worldwide; the deepest-diving mammal
- Q803927 Baird's beaked whale | in the deep North Pacific

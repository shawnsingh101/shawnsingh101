# People

Lives of individuals, one deck per person, in `decks/People/<field>/`. Each life is filed once and listed in the
subjects it belongs to with `shelves:` (Music, Art, Philosophy, Math, Science, History, Artificial intelligence…).

- **Faces come from the collection** `collections/People/0 Faces.md` (`kind: person`): portrait, birthplace with the
  flag of the time, own-language name. Add every new person there too; its headings are the movement they belong to
  ("Romantic music", "German philosophy"), not the folder, and People has no "By scene" lens.
- **The deck is a walk through the life.** Headings follow the chapters of the person's Wikipedia biography (Childhood,
  Paris, Majorca 1838–39, The last years); a heading never names a card's answer (use "Childhood", not "Prague").
- **A lesson first** (`:::lesson`): a short life in brief, a few paragraphs per chapter, with the fun stories (Chopin's
  winter at Valldemossa, Gödel's citizenship hearing). The cards then ask the places, people, works and turning points.
- **15–25 cards** a deck: places (with `related:` so the place's sheet lists the person), the people around them, the
  works, the turning points. Dates stay in the sentence. Names of places, people and works get `native:`.
- **Pictures of the place or thing the card is about**, right after that card, from Commons (check the file exists).
  Paintings still in copyright (Kline) are not copied; leave them out or hot-link the Wikipedia lead image.
- **Fair to the record**: leaders get their crimes and failures with the same care as their achievements (Mao's
  famine and Cultural Revolution; Oppenheimer's hearing).

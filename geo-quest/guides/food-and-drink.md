# Food & drink

- **Cocktails follow the owner's bartending course** (its study guide is the source of every measurement; the guide
  itself is never committed). Where the guide's spec differs from today's common pour (a very dry Manhattan, shaken
  Martinis), keep the guide's and say so in a `>` note.
- **Every drink is a recipe card** (```` ```recipe ````, see `decks/README.md`): what goes in, with amounts, as a glass
  that fills with colored blocks; then how it's served. Variations ("with vodka it's a Godmother") go in notes or
  one-line cloze cards, not recipes of their own.
- **Drinks named for an ingredient** (Gin and tonic, Tequila Sunrise): leave that ingredient out of the recipe and say
  "(after its shot of tequila)" in the prompt; the audit refuses a recipe whose ingredient is in the drink's name.
- **Name the drink** asks the reverse (ingredients → cocktail) under a neutral heading.
- Pictures: the drink's Wikipedia lead image when it's free (Commons), shown whole.

# Life (animals and plants)

- **The tree of life organizes; it isn't memorized** (`src/tree.ts`). No "name the branches" cards. It files Life's
  sections (Bacteria · Archaea · Eukaryotes › Animals › Mammals…), every group of species names its branch, and book
  decks about an animal feed it too.
- **One question, a rich answer.** An animal's card is its photo → its name. The back shows the scientific name (Latin
  speaker), its lineage as blocks from domain to species (`Lineage`), where it lives with a range map. Never ask what
  needs bones or teeth to see; look-alike tips go on the answer ("not to be confused with…"), not in a deck of their own.
- **Dinosaurs are asked when, not which.** No photo → name cards (book decks can ask more). Their card shows the life
  restoration and a fossil, gives the name and species, and asks the period, epoch and age; the back draws the
  time-scale blocks. **When it lived follows its Wikipedia infobox** (read by the nightly sync); the list line's range
  is only the fallback, and `fossilRange` in `collection-fixes.json` overrides both. They also show in Earth history
  (one deck, so its name never gives the period away).
- Not a children's set: no cow-dog-tiger basics.

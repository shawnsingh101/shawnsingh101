# Music

- **Recordings**: only free ones from Wikimedia Commons or Musopen (the music is old, but each recording has its own
  rights). Play the MP3 copy first (iPhones can't play Ogg).
- A clip is 30 seconds and starts at the moment people know (`@seconds`), not at the first bar; the reveal names the
  piece and part and shows the whole work. Anyone can move a clip's start while it plays (Start here).
- Pieces still under copyright (Copland, Prokofiev, Barber, Orff, Khachaturian, Shostakovich) can't be included.

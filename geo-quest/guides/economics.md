# Economics

- **One section per course**, following its syllabus lecture by lecture: Microeconomics for Public Policy,
  Macroeconomics for Public Policy, International Trade, International Capital Markets, International Finance and
  Monetary Theory, Theory of International Political Economy, Quantitative Analysis I and II. The owner's class notes
  are the guide; lessons and cards are in our own words, in the course's notation. Notes are never committed (the repo
  is public). Each lecture: a lesson, cards, and a "going further" deck built from the course's past exams.
- **Not FSO preparation.** It serves the owner's Foreign Service (economic cone) prep, but stays a general course.
- **The equation ladder**, for each important equation, in order: its derivation (stepped; one card blanks a step, for
  understanding), the formula (blank a term), inputs and outputs (what moves it, which way), its graph (a shock: which
  curve shifts, which way; the flip animates it), and a policy scenario. Tag each with `eq:`.
- **Every equation is explained**: each `eq:` name has one definition in `equations/<Subject>.md` (formula, every
  symbol, a worked example with real numbers, computed before it's written). A new equation means a new definition in
  the same change (audited).
- **Check derivations step by step**: each line follows from the one before, the "why" describes that step, and
  superscripts and subscripts match the words (nominal vs. real, 1 vs. 2).
- **Math is KaTeX**, bundled; the audit renders every formula. Numbers in "going further" cards are computed.
- **Next**: typed math answers (MathLive), checked by the app's Python (SymPy), for problem sets.

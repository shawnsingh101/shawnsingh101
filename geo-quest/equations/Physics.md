## Vector components
A_x = A\cos\theta,\qquad A_y = A\sin\theta
- A | magnitude of the vector
- \theta | angle measured counterclockwise from the positive x-axis
- A_x | x-component
- A_y | y-component
example: A vector of magnitude 5 at $\theta=\arctan(4/3)\approx53.13^\circ$ has $A_x=5\cos\theta\approx3$ and $A_y=5\sin\theta\approx4$ (rounded to the nearest hundredth).

## Vector magnitude
|\mathbf{A}| = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2}
- \mathbf{A} | vector in two dimensions
- A_x | x-component
- A_y | y-component
example: If $A_x=3$ and $A_y=4$, then $|\mathbf{A}|=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$.

## Dot product
\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B} = AB\cos\theta = A_xB_x + A_yB_y + A_zB_z
- \mathbf{A},\mathbf{B} | vectors
- A,B | their magnitudes
- \theta | angle between the vectors
- A_x,A_y,A_z | components of \mathbf{A}
- B_x,B_y,B_z | components of \mathbf{B}
example: For $\mathbf{A}=(3,4,0)$ and $\mathbf{B}=(2,1,0)$, $\mathbf{A}\cdot\mathbf{B}=3(2)+4(1)=10$.

## Cross product magnitude
|\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{B}| = AB\sin\theta
- \mathbf{A},\mathbf{B} | vectors
- A,B | their magnitudes
- \theta | angle between the vectors
example: If $A=4$, $B=5$ and $\theta=30^\circ$, then $|\mathbf{A}\times\mathbf{B}|=4(5)\sin(30^\circ)=10$.
